Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about exploring designs, submitting a booking request, and getting help with your project.

What is DreamDecor?

DreamDecor is an interior design inspiration platform where you can explore room styles, view before-and-after concepts, preview ideas, and submit design booking requests.

How do I book an interior design?

Open Explore, select a design, and choose the booking option. Complete the form with your contact details, room type, preferred consultation date, address, budget, and requirements.

Does submitting a booking mean my appointment is confirmed?

A booking request records your project details. Unless your confirmation page explicitly states that the appointment is confirmed, treat it as a request awaiting follow-up.

How much will my interior design project cost?

Prices shown for designs are estimates. Your final cost may depend on the room, measurements, materials, requirements, and scope of the project.

Can I choose a preferred consultation date?

Yes. You can enter your preferred date in the booking form. The date is a preference and may require confirmation.

Where can I see my booking requests?

Open My Profile to view your saved demo booking requests. Database-backed order history will appear there when the backend order endpoint is configured for your account.

What if I need to change my booking details?

Contact the DreamDecor support team with your name, email, booking reference if available, and the changes you need.

How do I report a problem or suggest an improvement?

Visit Contact Us, enter your name, email, mobile number, select a help category, and describe your issue or suggestion.

Can I use the website without logging in?

You can browse the public design pages. Features that depend on your account or saved data may require you to log in.

Still need help?

Send us your question or suggestion and our team can review it.
